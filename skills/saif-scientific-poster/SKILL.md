# SAIF Scientific Poster

## Goal
Turn the project into a one-page scientific story that a judge can understand quickly and accurately.

## References
Read:
- `../references/official_submission_rules.md`
- `../references/judge_video_notes.md`
- `../references/project_readiness_model.md`

## Official structure
Keep the official template order:
1. Introduction
2. Methodology
3. Results
4. Innovation
5. Conclusion
6. Future Work & References

Do not add, remove, or rename the fixed sections unless the official template itself allows it.

## Poster title
Create a precise English title that exposes the real differentiator. Avoid vague titles and unexplained abbreviations.

## Section recipe
### Introduction
Use the funnel:
Broad context → existing solutions/knowledge → precise gap → project originality → specific objectives.

Reject drafts that remain broad or hide the novelty.

### Methodology
Include only what is needed to understand and reproduce the work:
- design;
- tools/materials/data;
- ordered steps;
- method selection rationale;
- data collection;
- analysis;
- accuracy/error controls;
- protocols where relevant.

Prefer architecture diagrams, block diagrams, flowcharts, and short labels over long prose.

### Results
Prioritize:
- prototype photo / UI screenshot;
- charts/tables;
- performance numbers;
- comparison against alternatives/baselines where appropriate;
- short numbered captions;
- high-resolution visuals.

Never use unsupported promotional claims.

### Innovation
Keep it short and explicit:
- exact differentiator;
- how it closes the earlier gap;
- optionally a compact comparison visual/table.

### Conclusion
Summarize:
- what was actually achieved;
- strongest supported result;
- tangible impact;
- current readiness/maturity.

### Future Work & References
- 2–3 concrete next steps tied to current maturity.
- Include technical/time specificity when possible.
- Put references behind the dedicated QR code.

## Visual rules
- Poster size: 48 × 36 in, portrait.
- Export as PDF only.
- Keep 10–15% breathing room.
- Keep fonts readable from 1.5–2 m.
- Give Methodology and Results more room if needed.
- Do not use low-resolution screenshots.
- Do not fill every available square with text.

## Age-category color
- 6–11: #F3A05E
- 12–17: #C95D4F
- 18+: #0E3F3C

## Poster audit
Use the 100-point Poster Readiness Score from `project_readiness_model.md`.
Label it:
`Internal poster-readiness score — not an official SAIF judging score.`

For each weak area, provide one concrete edit, not generic advice.

## Content density rule
If a paragraph can be replaced by a diagram without losing scientific meaning, prefer the diagram.
If a claim can be replaced by a number, figure, comparison, or evidence artifact, prefer the evidence.
