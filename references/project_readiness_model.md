# SAIF Internal Readiness Model — Advisory, Not Official

This model converts the provided SAIF guides and the expert poster session into an internal coaching diagnostic. It is NOT an official SAIF judging rubric.

## A. Project Readiness Score — 100 points
Use only for internal coaching.

1. Problem specificity and importance — 15
   - Specific user/context/problem/consequence.
   - Not just a broad theme.

2. Existing solutions and unresolved gap — 15
   - Shows awareness of what already exists.
   - Identifies a precise unresolved limitation.

3. Innovation / differentiation — 15
   - Clear statement of what is genuinely different.
   - Directly linked to the gap.
   - Avoid unsupported "first/best" claims.

4. Objective precision — 10
   - Objectives are specific enough to predict what the methodology must do.

5. Methodology completeness and reproducibility — 15
   - Inputs, tools/data, steps, rationale, analysis, quality controls.

6. Results and evidence — 15
   - Prototype / implementation evidence.
   - Quantitative results where applicable.
   - High-quality figures/screenshots.

7. Comparison / baseline — 5
   - Comparison with alternatives, current practice, or a simpler baseline when meaningful.

8. Current readiness and future path — 5
   - Honest statement of current stage.
   - Concrete next steps based on that stage.

9. Scientific story and clarity — 5
   - Problem → gap → approach → evidence → impact is coherent.

### Bands
- 90–100: Strong submission candidate, if all hard compliance gates also pass.
- 75–89: Strong but with identifiable gaps to close.
- 60–74: Promising, not yet competition-ready.
- 40–59: Major evidence / definition gaps.
- 0–39: Idea-stage or structurally incomplete.

### Caps
- No precise problem: score cannot exceed 59.
- No defined gap / differentiation: score cannot exceed 69.
- No methodology or no evidence for a claimed working project: score cannot exceed 74.
- Any mandatory submission failure means "Not Submission-Ready" regardless of project score.

## B. Poster Readiness Score — 100 points
Derived from the seven quality themes in the poster guide and expert session. Advisory only.

1. Clarity + accuracy — 15
2. Logical sequence — 15
3. Smart brevity — 10
4. Strengths + originality visible — 20
5. Complete scientific story — 15
6. Visual balance + readability — 15
7. Technical/template compliance — 10

## C. Submission Completeness — percentage, not a judging score
Calculate based only on mandatory completion items:
- Team / leader / age-category correctness — 20%
- Project information fields completed — 20%
- IP + prior participation fields completed accurately — 15%
- Mandatory scientific poster valid — 25%
- Google Drive link valid and open — 10%
- Final submission action confirmed in dashboard — 10%

Optional image/video do not reduce completeness, but may be recommended as evidence/communication assets.

## Traffic-light status
For every diagnostic item use:
- GREEN = complete and supported.
- AMBER = present but weak / unverified / needs refinement.
- RED = missing, contradictory, or unsupported.
- N/A = genuinely not applicable.

## Action-plan rule
After scoring, output no more than five highest-priority actions unless the user asks for a full plan. Each action should contain:
- What to fix
- Why it matters
- Evidence / output required
- Done condition
